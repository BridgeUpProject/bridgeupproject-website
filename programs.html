AI Pathways

An eight-week, small-group workshop that introduces teens to AI as a practical tool. Each session starts with something familiar and builds toward a skill they can use immediately. Sessions may be adapted to fit each cohort's needs.

The eight sessions

In order. Each one ends with something the group has actually made or can actually do.

1

What is AI?

  • Introduction to AI through fun, relatable activities
  • The Jordan vs. LeBron debate, settled with AI
  • Build a fantasy football team
  • Generate songs, images, and creative content
2

Talking to AI: Prompting

  • How to ask better questions
  • Using voice conversations with AI
  • Getting useful answers for real-life situations
  • Building confidence with AI tools
3

Literacy & Communication

  • Reading and comprehension support
  • Writing stronger emails, messages, and responses
  • Grammar, vocabulary, and communication skills
  • Voice conversations with AI
4

Your AI Advisor

  • Career exploration
  • Goal setting and planning
  • Finding community resources
  • Navigating challenges and informed decisions
5

Making Money with AI

  • Exploring side hustles and small businesses
  • Braiding, nails, car washing, tutoring, and more
  • Using AI to generate business ideas
  • Identifying personal strengths and skills
6

Finding Your First Customer

  • Creating flyers and marketing materials
  • Writing texts and DMs to potential customers
  • Social media content and promotion
  • Building a simple business plan
7

Understanding Your Money

  • Understanding a paycheck
  • Understanding taxes and deductions
  • Creating a basic budget with AI
  • Setting savings goals and tracking progress
8

Life Skills & Independence

  • Bills and due dates
  • Banking basics
  • Credit and savings
  • Using AI to solve everyday problems
