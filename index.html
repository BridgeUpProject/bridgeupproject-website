Our Mission

Flagship Program

AI Pathways

An eight-week, small-group workshop that introduces teens to AI as a practical tool. Each session starts with something familiar and builds toward a skill they can use immediately.

  • Talking to AI: Prompting
  • Making Money with AI
  • Understanding Your Money
  • Life Skills & Independence
